About

Our mission

Savings & Investment Pro is an independent financial education site. It offers free calculators (loans, savings, retirement, buy vs rent, time value of money, NPV/IRR) to help anyone understand how rates, inflation and time affect their finances, in Tunisia, North Africa, Europe and beyond.

Methodology

Formulas are standard: constant payment loans, monthly compound interest, APR solved as the actuarial rate on real cash flows, and real return through the Fisher relation.

Policy rates come from central banks' official websites (BCT, Bank of Algeria, CBL, Bank Al-Maghrib, ECB, Fed). Each value is dated and linked to its source. Assumptions (inflation, returns, price growth) are entered by the user: they are scenarios, not forecasts.
